  NOTE FROM THE KEEPER
  -------------------------------------------------------------
  Read this once, and then read nothing else in this room until
  you have understood it.

  The plate room does not write on plates. When the plate room
  puts something INTO a plate it goes in as a WORKS ENTRY, and a
  works entry is always the same shape:

        PL8     the works stamp, three characters
        .       one character, the issue of the stamp
        ..      the length of the entry, two characters, low first
        ....    a check over the entry, four characters, low first
        ....    the entry itself

  The check is the ordinary one. Any clerk can run it.

  If you have pulled something out of a plate and it does not
  begin with the stamp, and the check over it does not come out,
  then it is not a works entry. It is somebody's handwriting.

  This room is full of handwriting. None of it is worth the paper
  and some of it was left to be found.

  There is nothing on this board that will tell you whether an
  answer is right. The stamp and the check are the only two
  things that ever will. Learn to trust them and you will not
  waste a week.

  The keeper, 11th of the month
