TELEPHONE ROOM - LINE 4, PAIR 2 - ENGINEER'S NOTE
=================================================

  Both recordings off the same pair, same night, same gear.

  UNDERTONE is forty seconds of the line with nobody on it. The
  right leg is tone and nothing but tone, and I would not spend
  your evening on it, though somebody has been at it and you will
  find what they left.

  The left leg is where the work is.

  You will not find the message in any one part of the left leg,
  because it is not in any one part of it. It is laid down the
  whole length, thin enough to sit under the noise, and the noise
  is louder than it is. Looking harder at any second of it will
  not help you. Adding a great many seconds of it together, with
  the right thing to add them against, will.

  What you add it against comes off the card, in the ordinary
  way, from the run before this one.

  LINETEST is the same gear, the same trick, the same everything,
  cut short. It is the reason I kept it. It is keyed on its own
  name and nothing else - the two words LINE TEST, capitals, one
  space between them, and the card's rule applied to that instead
  of to a seal.

  What the line test says is no secret, because a line test that
  says something secret is no use to anybody. It says:

        NULL0RIGIN LINE TEST 1 OF 1

  as a works entry, stamp and check and all.

  So you have a recording, and you have its key, and you have
  what it is supposed to say. If you cannot get from those three
  to how the trick is done, the long one will not help you.

  Work out the trick on the short one. Then go and do it to the
  long one with the proper key.
