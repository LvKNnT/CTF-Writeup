COMPOSING ROOM - FORME 3 - LAST NIGHT'S WORK
============================================

  I will say it once more and then I am done with saying it.

  Nothing is hid in the forme. Read the forme. Read it letter by
  letter, hold it to the light, pull a proof and read the proof.
  You will get the forme and the forme is the forme.

  A compositor has choices. That is the only thing about him that
  is worth anything.

  Here is the choice. When the sort he wants next is one he has
  ALREADY SET, and it is still back there in the stick where he
  can reach it, he may take it from there - or he may go to the
  case and set it fresh. Both give the same line. Both read the
  same on the proof. No reader alive can tell you which he did by
  looking at the page.

  But he can tell you. And I can tell you. And the man who set
  this forme knew that I could.

  THREE CHARACTERS MAKE A LIGATURE. Never two, never four. If he
  reached back, he reached back for three.

  AND HE ALWAYS REACHED FOR THE NEAREST ONE. Every time. He never
  went hunting up the stick for an older copy when a newer one
  was to hand. A tidy man.

  Where he had no such choice - where the sort was not back there
  at all - he went to the case, and that means nothing, and you
  should read nothing into it.

  So: walk the forme the way he set it. At every point where he
  COULD have reached back, note whether he did. That is the
  message. Everywhere else is just a man setting type.

  The card does the rest, off the run before.

  DO NOT RESET THE FORME. I should not have to write that down.
  A forme reset is a forme set by somebody else, and somebody
  else had nothing to say.

  He went home at six and did not come in the Monday.
