DYE HOUSE - BATH 5 - WORKING NOTES
==================================

  ON THE PAGE

  Nothing is written on this page. You may read every word on it
  and every dot of every word and you will have read the page,
  which is all it was ever for.

  But a page has to be LAID DOWN before it can be rolled up, and
  it is laid down one line at a time, and there are five ways to
  lay a line, and which of the five was ours to choose. We chose
  every line of this page. Nineteen hundred lines and not one of
  them chosen for the reason you would think.

  Four of the five ways say something. The fifth says the same as
  the first and we used it when we wanted the sheet to look like
  everybody else's sheet.

  Read the page as often as you like. Do not COPY IT OUT. A page
  written out a second time is written the copier's way, line by
  line, in the copier's habits, and every choice we made is gone
  and the page still reads and still looks like a page.

  ON THE MORDANT

  A mordant is not one thing. Ours is SIX.

  They go into the bath in the order they were drawn, first to
  last, and the bath does not take until all six are in, and if
  one of them is wrong the bath does not half take - it does
  nothing whatever, and you will not be able to tell from looking
  whether you were close.

  The works has been handing you the six one at a time since the
  first sleeve. Every entry you have opened on this board carried
  its seal, and then a separator, and then eight octets that were
  not the seal and that nothing asked you to keep.

  You will have thrown them away. Most do. Go back and get them.
  They are still there. The order is the order you took them in.

  The card's key opens the page. The six open what is on it.
  Neither one alone opens anything, which is the entire point of
  a mordant and the reason this house has lasted.

  ON THE SEVENTH

  There is a seventh share on these premises. It is lying where
  anybody would find it, in the first place anybody would look,
  inside a perfectly good works entry with a perfectly good check
  over it.

  We did not use it. Neither should you.
