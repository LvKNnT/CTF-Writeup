SLEEVE 9 - CONTACT SHEET - 2048 FRAMES
======================================

  Sixty-four across, thirty-two down, read the way you read
  anything: along the top row first.

  They were shot IN PAIRS. Frame nought and frame one are a pair,
  frame two and frame three are a pair, and so on to the end of
  the sheet. Each frame of a pair carries one bit and the two
  bits disagree, always, by design. One of the two is the truth
  and the other was put there to be found.

  Nothing in the picture tells you which. The pictures are all
  real exposures and all worthless.

  Every frame carries its own SEAL, and the seal is what sorts
  them. A frame's seal is struck over the frame's own NUMBER and
  the bit it carries, with the die from the run before this one -

        seal = SHA-256( k, then the frame number as two octets
               high first, then the one octet that is the bit )

  and only the first two octets of that are kept, because two
  octets is enough to sort a sheet and not so many that the sheet
  looks written on.

  k is what the card says it is.

  Strike the seal yourself for both frames of a pair. One will
  come out. Take that frame's bit and throw the other away. Do
  that a thousand times and you have the run.

  Read the sheet straight through without sorting it and you will
  get something. You will get a great deal, in fact. All of it
  was put there for the man who does not sort.

  The whole of the trick is that the chaff is not disguised. It
  is not hidden, it is not weak, it is not badly made. It is
  perfectly good chaff and there is more of it than there is
  grain, and without the die there is no reading on this earth
  that will separate them.
