DARKROOM - DISH 2 - ELEVEN EXPOSURES
====================================

  Eleven frames of the one plate coming up.

  Somebody has been at the frames themselves. You will find what
  they left there soon enough, and when you have it you will have
  nothing, and you will have spent an evening getting it.

  The frames are not where this is kept. Stack them, difference
  them, pull them apart to the last dot - you will be taking
  apart a picture of a plate coming up in a dish, which is all it
  has ever been.

  What is not the same from frame to frame is the TRAY.

  The colourman sets his tray out before he starts. He has his
  colours and he lays them in the slots, and he is particular
  that they are all different colours, and he is particular that
  there are the full two hundred and fifty six of them, and past
  that he is not particular at all.

  There is an order they OUGHT to lie in. Any colourman will tell
  you what it is: darkest to lightest, by the plain reckoning,
  red before green before blue.

  Ours does not lay them in that order.

  He works two to a slot and he is careless which of the two goes
  on the left. He has been careless in the same way every day for
  four years, which is a great deal of carelessness for one man,
  and every frame in this dish has its own tray.

  When a colour moves, the picture does not, because he moves the
  picture with it. That is the trade. A man who could not do that
  would not be a colourman.

  The card, as ever, says which way round the carelessness reads.

  One more thing, and I would not say it if it had not already
  cost somebody a week. Do not let anybody tidy the tray. Any
  decent hand who picks these frames up will put the colours back
  the way they OUGHT to lie, because that is what a decent hand
  does, and the frames will look exactly the same afterwards, and
  there will be nothing left in them at all.
